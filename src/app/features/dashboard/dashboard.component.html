monetization_on How much does ACME spend on salaries?

Annual payroll commitment across the 15-employee sample is structured by region: $410,000 USD in the US, £187,000 GBP in the UK, €333,000 EUR across Europe, ¥25.5M JPY in Japan, ₹9.3M INR in India, and $140,000 CAD in Canada.

bar_chart What is the typical salary?

The median annual compensation (which removes high-earner skew) stands at $135,000 USD in North America, €88,000 EUR in Continental Europe, and ₹3,200,000 INR in India.

public Which countries have higher / lower compensation?

The United States leads with an average annual compensation of $136,667 USD, followed by the United Kingdom (£93,500 GBP). Continental European locations (Germany, France, Spain) average €83,250 EUR.

business Which departments have higher / lower average salaries?

Engineering and Product departments command higher average compensation across all operating regions, driven by specialized technical architectural roles, while HR and Finance exhibit steady, standardized compensation structures.
